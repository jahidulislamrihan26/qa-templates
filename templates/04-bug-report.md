## Steps to reproduce
Numbered, exact, starting from a clean state. Someone who has never seen the product must be able to repeat them.
1. <Go to ...>

## Expected result
What should happen, and why (requirement, specification or common sense).

## Actual result
What happened, in observable terms: the message, the number, the page, the status code.

## Evidence
Screenshot, short video, console log, request and response. Attach, do not describe.

## Impact
Who is affected and what it costs (money, data, security, legal, trust). This is what lets someone decide the priority.

## Notes
Workaround, how often it happens, related defects, anything not yet verified.
